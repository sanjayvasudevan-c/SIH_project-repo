What this prototype demonstrates

Approval cases need clear ownership, visible deadlines, explainable assignment, and a human decision record. This prototype models those workflow controls. It does not make approval decisions, and its people, applications, and benchmark records are synthetic.

Method and limits

The benchmark seeds 15,000 synthetic applications, runs the deadline engine twice, then sends 400 requests to each read screen at concurrency 20. The first cycle has setup work; the second cycle checks repeat behavior. Runs were made at different commits on the same reported four-core host and are available separately for inspection.

Both engine cycles in the 4 November run took longer than the demo’s one-minute interval. The two runs are not a controlled before/after experiment. They do not establish production, multi-region, or real AICTE workload performance.
